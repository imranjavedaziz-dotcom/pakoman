FROM INTEREST TO INFORMED ACTION

Good partnerships
begin with good questions.

  1. Define the opportunity. Identify the product, market, customer and delivery model.
  2. Check the requirements. Verify incorporation, sector licences, customs, standards and tax obligations with the competent authorities.
  3. Know your counterparty. Validate registration, ownership, references and payment arrangements.
  4. Build a clear agreement. Seek qualified advice on contract terms, dispute resolution and cross-border obligations.
