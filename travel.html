Passport categories matter.

An arrangement concerning diplomatic, special or official passports must not be treated as an exemption for ordinary passport holders. Do not book travel on the basis of a headline: confirm eligibility and effective dates with the issuing authority and airline.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you go

Does this website process visa applications?

No. Apply through the linked official government portal. We do not collect passport details, payments or application documents.

Are fees and processing times fixed?

They can change by category and circumstances. Confirm them on the official application system before paying or making non-refundable arrangements.

Where should I get consular help?

Find the relevant embassy or consulate through Pakistan MOFA or the Oman Foreign Ministry. Save contact details before departure.
