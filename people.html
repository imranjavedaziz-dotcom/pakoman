BEFORE YOU APPLY

A little preparation goes a long way.

How do I find a genuine scholarship?

Use an official university or ministry announcement. Confirm the application deadline, eligibility, funding coverage and application domain. This portal does not advertise a currently open bilateral scholarship.

Will my qualification be recognised?

Ask the receiving institution and the relevant recognition authority. Academic recognition and professional licensing are separate processes; neither should be assumed.

Can healthcare professionals work across borders?

Practice requires compliance with the destination’s current registration, examination, employment and immigration requirements. Contact the competent professional regulator before making arrangements.

Are exchange programs open now?

The friendship concepts on this website are proposals, not active recruitment. Confirm any real opportunity with its named organiser before sharing documents or making payments.
